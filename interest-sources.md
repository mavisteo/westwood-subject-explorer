# Interests guide — source and matching notes

Checked 6 November 2026. This is a local, rule-based guided activity, adapted from the user's supplied Path B flow. It does not call or impersonate the linked Custom GPT. The GPT conversation was inaccessible to web retrieval; the supplied instructions define the interaction. Students can open the public GPT separately.

The mandatory subject-level filter takes precedence over the later inconsistent mention of JC/MI: G3 → Polytechnic only; G2 → Polytechnic/ITE; G1 → ITE only. G2 permits both ITE and Polytechnic matches; suitability comes before institution quotas. Grades are not collected, so results are interest matches, with admission requirements linked for checking.

The curated catalogue contains 35 Polytechnic diplomas (31 RP, two SP, one NYP and one NIEC/NP) and 25 three-year Higher Nitec courses. It is a starting selection, not a complete CourseFinder mirror. Area labels and plain-language summaries are editorial. CCA-derived strengths are tentative and students choose whether they fit. Explicit career interests, subjects enjoyed and selected strengths guide area ranking; disliked subjects lower matching scores. Dream-job phrases also prioritise relevant course titles/content. Selection is restricted to chosen areas and permitted institutions, with distinct course IDs. Individual course-content topics now drive the shortlist. Named career interests take priority; otherwise explicit career fields, CCA topics and liked subjects provide positive evidence. Disliked subjects lower a match and receive a visible note where relevant. Generic strengths only support an existing link. Recommendations are ranked by relevance, without forcing breadth across areas or institution quotas. The shortlist contains up to three sufficiently close matches; zero, one or two matches are explicitly labelled when there are fewer suitable courses in this starter catalogue.

## Score provenance

Every record in `dist/interests-data.json` carries an explicit year, exercise, aggregate type, college-by-college score/range, source label and source URL. There is no runtime score scraping or fabricated “live” score.

- MOE CourseFinder supplied 2025 JAE ranges for R24 (10–19, ELR2B2-D) and R28 (16–26, ELR2B2-B).
- MOE CourseFinder supplied 2025 JIE ‘N’ ranges for HF3SM (Central 2–4, East 2–5, West 2–6; ELB3), HF3BW (East 2–5; B4), HF3FM (East 3–15, West 3–12; B4), and 2026 HF3CN (Central 2–5, East 2–8, West 2–9; MAS1B3).
- Most other MOE CourseFinder pages returned robot-verification pages. These cards label their fallback score sources explicitly.
- RP's official [2026 JAE intake table](https://www.rp.edu.sg/admissions/intake/) supplies the other RP net ELR2B2 ranges and aggregate types.
- [MOE's 2026 JAE course booklet](https://www.moe.gov.sg/-/media/files/post-secondary/2026-jae/2026-jae-courses.pdf), Humanities table, supplies N96's 2025 JAE ELR2B2-A range (6–19).
- [ITE's official 2026 JIE ‘N’ entry booklet](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/145/097d74ce-8353-4a03-b0b1-f12b5822d473/JIEN_JIEE%202026%20Intake%20entry%20requirements.pdf), pp. 1–3, supplies 2025 last-posted ITE aggregate points for the other Higher Nitec courses. These are labelled **last posted scores**, not invented ranges.
- [ITE's current three-year course directory](https://www.ite.edu.sg/secondary-school-students/ite-full-time-courses/3-year-4-year-higher-nitec/) confirms current course names and school-leaver routes. ITE's [transition announcement](https://www.ite.edu.sg/about-ite/newsroom/news-releases-and-speeches/ite-introduces-seven-new-three-year-higher-nitec-courses-in-2026/) confirms the completed Nitec → three-year Higher Nitec transition in 2026. Retired school-leaver Nitec routes are not recommended.

Scores do not establish individual admission eligibility. Past scores and aggregate systems are not interchangeable. The UI explains that JAE scores refer to O-Level admission and that PFP uses a different aggregate; G2 students must check PFP/qualifying O-Level routes. Full subject grades, medical/aptitude/interview and professional conditions remain on the linked official pages.

CourseFinder detail links use the site's course-key query format, and the result footer also provides CourseFinder search by name/code in case a detail URL moves. Retrieval verified only a subset of details due to the access limitation above.
